================================================================================
WHAT THIS PIPELINE DOES
================================================================================

1. DE NOVO VARIANT DETECTION
   ---------------------------
   Identifies new mutations that occurred in the child but are not present
   in either parent. These are highest priority for autism genetics.

   Method:
   - Proband genotype: 0/1 (heterozygous)
   - Mother genotype:  0/0 (homozygous reference)
   - Father genotype:  0/0 (homozygous reference)
   - Quality: Phred ≥30, Coverage ≥20x in all trio members

   Expected: 50-100 high-confidence de novo variants

2. RARE INHERITED VARIANT DETECTION
   ---------------------------------
   Identifies rare functional variants transmitted from parents that may
   contribute to autism risk through recessive or compound effects.

   Method:
   - Population frequency: MAF ≤0.01 (1%)
   - Databases: 1000 Genomes, ESP6500, ExAC
   - Functional: Nonsynonymous variants only
   - Inheritance: Present in child AND at least one parent
   - Quality: Phred ≥30, Coverage ≥20x in all trio members

   Categorized by:
   - Maternal inheritance
   - Paternal inheritance
   - Compound heterozygous (from both parents)
   - Homozygous recessive

   Expected: 100-500 rare inherited variants

PIPELINE SCRIPTS
================================================================================

MAIN SCRIPTS:
-------------

1. complete_autism_analysis.sh [MASTER SCRIPT - USE THIS]
   - Runs the entire pipeline from start to finish
   - Orchestrates all steps
   - Generates comprehensive reports
   - Runtime: 8-12 hours

2. convert_sra_fixed.sh
   - Downloads FASTQ files from ENA (not SRA!)
   - Avoids SRA Toolkit reference dependency issues
   - Runtime: 10 minutes

3. preprocess_trio_optimized.sh
   - Alignment with BWA-MEM
   - Duplicate marking
   - Base quality recalibration (BQSR)
   - Runtime: 4-6 hours

4. complete_trio_pipeline.sh
   - Variant calling with GATK HaplotypeCaller
   - Joint genotyping
   - De novo variant detection
   - Runtime: 2-4 hours

5. rare_inherited_variants_pipeline.sh
   - Quality filtering (Phred ≥30, DP ≥20x)
   - Annotation with ANNOVAR
   - Population frequency filtering (MAF ≤0.01)
   - Functional filtering (nonsynonymous)
   - Inheritance pattern analysis
   - Runtime: 1-2 hours

SUPPORTING SCRIPTS:

6. run_denovo_analysis.sh
