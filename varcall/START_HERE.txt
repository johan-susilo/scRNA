TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------

Q: wget fails with "connection refused"?
A: Your server might block FTP. Try Method 1 (robust downloader)

Q: fasterq-dump is very slow?
A: Normal for large files. Use 'screen' to run in background:
   screen -S download
   ./download_trio_robust.sh
   [Ctrl+A, D to detach]

Q: Out of disk space?
A: Each sample needs ~20-30 GB. Total: ~180 GB for trio
   Check: df -h ~
